# 延伸閱讀：學術寫作與出版中的 AI 責任歸屬與認識論風險

起點文獻：
- van Zoonen, Morgan-Thomas & Tursunbayeva (2026). Beyond AI disclosure: Claim accountability and responsible research in scholarly publishing. *European Management Journal*, 44, 550–556. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.emj.2026.06.001
- Tang, B. L. (2025). The epistemic downside of using LLM-based generative AI in academic writing. *Publications*, 13(4), 63. https://doi.org/10.3390/publications13040063

兩篇的交集：不只問「有沒有揭露 AI」，而是問 AI 進入學術知識生產後，誰能為主張負責、研究者的判斷與理解是否被掏空。以下依主題分組，所有 DOI 皆經 Scite / Consensus 資料庫核對，未見撤稿紀錄（Gerlich 2025 有一則表格勘誤；Kaebnick 2023 有一則 erratum）。

## A. 責任歸屬、作者身分與期刊治理（最接近 van Zoonen et al.）

- Lindebaum, D., & Fleming, P. (2024). ChatGPT undermines human reflexivity, scientific responsibility and responsible management research. *British Journal of Management*, 35(2), 566–575. https://doi.org/10.1111/1467-8551.12781 — van Zoonen 反覆引用的核心對話對象，主張 AI 對其產出「無利害」（no stake），與科學責任不相容。
- Hosseini, M., Resnik, D. B., & Holmes, K. (2023). The ethics of disclosing the use of artificial intelligence tools in writing scholarly manuscripts. *Research Ethics*, 19(4), 449–465. https://doi.org/10.1177/17470161231180449 — 揭露倫理的奠基論文，主張禁令不可行，AI 不能負道德責任故不可為作者。
- Lin, Z. (2024). Towards an AI policy framework in scholarly publishing. *Trends in Cognitive Sciences*. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tics.2023.12.002 — 提出比「揭露」更細緻的政策框架，認知科學社群讀者尤其相關。
- Flanagin, A., et al. (2023). Nonhuman "authors" and implications for the integrity of scientific publication and medical knowledge. *JAMA*. https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.2023.1344
- Kaebnick, G. E., et al. (2023). Editors' statement on the responsible use of generative AI technologies in scholarly journal publishing. *Hastings Center Report*. https://doi.org/10.1002/hast.1507（同文另刊於 *AJOB*, https://doi.org/10.1080/15265161.2023.2292437）
- Crawford, J., et al. (2026). Authorship statement for generative artificial intelligence: Assuring trust and accountability. *Journal of University Teaching and Learning Practice*. https://doi.org/10.53761/v16abt43 — 明文寫出「責任不可從具名作者移轉」，與 claim accountability 立場高度呼應。
- LaFlamme, M., & Meyers, N. (2026). Enacting AI disclosure in scholarly publishing. *American Ethnologist*. https://doi.org/10.1111/amet.70084
- Johnson, R. F. (2026). Provenance, not prohibition: A framework for AI in scholarly publishing. *Laryngoscope Investigative Otolaryngology*. https://doi.org/10.1002/lio2.70487
- Binz, M., et al. (2025). How should the advancement of large language models affect the practice of science? *PNAS*. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2401227121 — 四組學者的正反辯論，Botvinick & Gershman 主張人類須保留科學路線圖的責任。
- Colangelo, M., et al. (2026). The sophist in the server. *EMBO Reports*. https://doi.org/10.1038/s44319-026-00711-w — 修辭上的可信與證據支持的混淆。

政策實證盤點（若需引用「期刊實際怎麼規定」）：
- Ganjavi, C., et al. (2024). Publishers' and journals' instructions to authors on use of generative AI in academic and scientific publishing. *BMJ*. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj-2023-077192
- Perkins, M., et al. (2023). Academic publisher guidelines on AI usage: A ChatGPT supported thematic analysis. *F1000Research*. https://doi.org/10.12688/f1000research.142411.2
- Yoo, J. H. (2025). Defining the boundaries of AI use in scientific writing: A comparative review of editorial policies. *J Korean Med Sci*. https://doi.org/10.3346/jkms.2025.40.e187
- Raitskaya, L., et al. (2025). The 2025 landscape of generative AI in scholarly writing and publishing: A scoping review. *Journal of Language and Education*. https://doi.org/10.17323/jle.2025.29876
- Nabavi, A., et al. (2026). Artificial intelligence in scholarly peer review: A scoping review of applications, risks, and governance. *Int J Med Inform*. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijmedinf.2026.106418 — 對應 van Zoonen 的 confidentiality threshold。

## B. 認識論風險：理解的錯覺、信任校準（最接近 Tang 的第一項）

- Messeri, L., & Crockett, M. J. (2024). Artificial intelligence and illusions of understanding in scientific research. *Nature*, 627, 49–58. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-07146-0 — Tang 的「illusion of understanding」直接源頭，必讀。
- Heersmink, R., et al. (2024). A phenomenology and epistemology of large language models: Transparency, trust, and trustworthiness. *Ethics and Information Technology*. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10676-024-09777-3
- Hila, A. (2025). The epistemological consequences of large language models: Rethinking collective intelligence and institutional knowledge. *AI & Society*. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00146-025-02426-3 — 論 LLM 只能提供 reliabilist 而非 reflective 的證成，規模化外包會侵蝕理解。
- Loru, E., et al. (2025). The simulation of judgment in LLMs. *PNAS*. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2518443122 — 提出 "epistemia"：表面可信取代驗證。
- Bottazzi Grifoni, E., et al. (2025). The bewitching AI: The illusion of communication with large language models. *Philosophy & Technology*. https://doi.org/10.1007/s13347-025-00893-6
- Kim, S. S. Y., et al. (2025). Fostering appropriate reliance on large language models. *CHI 2025*. https://doi.org/10.1145/3706598.3714020 — 實驗證據：解釋會提高對錯誤答案的依賴。
- Ibrahim, L., et al. (2025). Measuring and mitigating overreliance to build human-compatible AI. arXiv. https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2509.08010（預印本）
- Maynard, A. (2026). The AI cognitive Trojan horse: How LLMs may bypass human epistemic vigilance. arXiv. https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2601.07085（預印本）
- Abdurahman, S., et al. (2024). Perils and opportunities in using large language models in psychological research. *PNAS Nexus*. https://doi.org/10.1093/pnasnexus/pgae245 — 針對心理學研究的 "GPTology" 警告。

## C. 認知鈍化、認知卸載與依賴（最接近 Tang 的第二、三項）

- Lee, H.-P., Sarkar, A., Tankelevitch, L., et al. (2025). The impact of generative AI on critical thinking: Self-reported reductions in cognitive effort and confidence effects from a survey of knowledge workers. *CHI 2025*. https://doi.org/10.1145/3706598.3713778
- Gerlich, M. (2025). AI tools in society: Impacts on cognitive offloading and the future of critical thinking. *Societies*, 15(1), 6. https://doi.org/10.3390/soc15010006（有勘誤 10.3390/soc15090252；相關性設計，因果推論須謹慎）
- Fan, Y., et al. (2024). Beware of metacognitive laziness: Effects of generative AI on learning motivation, processes, and performance. *BJET*. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjet.13544
- Kosmyna, N., et al. (2025). Your brain on ChatGPT: Accumulation of cognitive debt when using an AI assistant for essay writing task. arXiv. https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2506.08872（預印本，EEG 研究；已有方法學評論 10.48550/arxiv.2601.00856，引用時宜一併看）
- Yan, L., Greiff, S., et al. (2025). Distinguishing performance gains from learning when using generative AI. *Nature Reviews Psychology*. https://doi.org/10.1038/s44159-025-00467-5
- Clark, A. (2025). Extending minds with generative AI. *Nature Communications*. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-59906-9 — 延伸心智取徑的反面聲音，可作平衡。
- Choudhury, A., & Chaudhry, Z. (2024). Large language models and user trust: Consequence of self-referential learning loop and the deskilling of health care professionals. *JMIR*. https://doi.org/10.2196/56764
- Izquierdo-Condoy, J. S., et al. (2025). Generative AI in medical education: Enhancing critical thinking or undermining cognitive...? *JMIR*. https://doi.org/10.2196/76340
- Delikoura, I., et al. (2025). From superficial outputs to superficial learning: Risks of LLMs in education (systematic review of 70 studies). arXiv. https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2509.21972（預印本）
