# 註解書目：學術寫作與出版中的 AI 責任歸屬與認識論風險

整理日期：2026-09-10

起點文獻為 van Zoonen, Morgan-Thomas & Tursunbayeva (2026) 與 Tang (2025)，兩篇的交集是：AI 進入學術知識生產後，誰能為主張負責，以及研究者的判斷與理解是否被掏空。以下依四大主題分組，每一主題內依重要性排序，最重要者標示 **【必讀】**。

書目格式為 APA 7。作者、卷期、頁碼取自 Crossref 與 arXiv 的官方紀錄，DOI 皆經 Scite 資料庫核對，未見撤稿；Gerlich (2025) 有一則表格勘誤、Kaebnick et al. (2023) 有一則 erratum，已註記。摘要依各篇官方摘要撰寫；五篇（Lin 2024、Messeri & Crockett 2024、Lee et al. 2025、Brown & Marsden 2026、Colangelo & Galli 2026）資料庫未提供完整摘要，摘要依我對該文的既有理解或簡短摘要撰寫，已於該條標註「未依官方摘要」，引用前請自行核對全文。

排序的依據是：與兩篇起點文獻的論證關係、在該主題中的奠基地位、以及被引用的廣度。這是我的判斷，不是客觀指標。

## 主題 A：責任歸屬、作者身分與期刊治理

對應 van Zoonen et al. 的 claim accountability 論證。

### A0. 起點文獻

van Zoonen, W., Morgan-Thomas, A., & Tursunbayeva, A. (2026). Beyond AI disclosure: Claim accountability and responsible research in scholarly publishing. *European Management Journal*, *44*(4), 550–556. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.emj.2026.06.001

- 中文：主張期刊的禁止、強制揭露、只看效度三種政策都在管制「工具的使用」而非「主張的可辯護性」，治理單位應從工具轉為主張：每一項進入學術紀錄的主張都須有具名的人能重建並辯護。提出雙門檻框架（confidentiality threshold 保護審查中的受託材料；judgment threshold 保護人對推理的責任），並轉譯為作者、審查者、編輯、出版者的角色自評。
- English: Argues that prohibition, mandatory disclosure and validity-only permissiveness all regulate tool use rather than the defensibility of claims; the unit of governance should be the claim, with a named human able to reconstruct and defend it. Proposes a two-threshold framework (confidentiality; judgment) and role-based self-assessments for authors, reviewers, editors and publishers.

### A1. 【必讀】

Lindebaum, D., & Fleming, P. (2024). ChatGPT undermines human reflexivity, scientific responsibility and responsible management research. *British Journal of Management*, *35*(2), 566–575. https://doi.org/10.1111/1467-8551.12781

- 中文：van Zoonen 等人反覆對話的核心文獻。作者分析 ChatGPT 的運作與其內在認識論限制，指出它以高機率、衍生性的選擇產生文本，對自己產出的知識「無利害」（no stake），因此傾向產出不負責任的內容；真正的人類思考則嵌於具體社會文化脈絡，能做出低機率、有創造性且有承諾的選擇。結論是 ChatGPT 的使用與科學責任及負責任的管理研究完全不相容。
- English: The core interlocutor for van Zoonen et al. Unpacks ChatGPT's intrinsic epistemological limits: it generates text through high-probability, derivative choices and has no stake in the knowledge it produces, making it prone to irresponsible output, whereas human thinking is embodied, contextual and committed. Concludes that ChatGPT use is incompatible with scientific responsibility and responsible management research.

### A2. 【必讀】

Hosseini, M., Resnik, D. B., & Holmes, K. (2023). The ethics of disclosing the use of artificial intelligence tools in writing scholarly manuscripts. *Research Ethics*, *19*(4), 449–465. https://doi.org/10.1177/17470161231180449

- 中文：AI 揭露倫理的奠基論文。主張禁令不可行且只會助長隱匿使用；LLM 沒有自由意志、無法負道德或法律責任，因此列為作者或放進致謝都不恰當，應如軟體工具般在文內引用。建議研究者在導論或方法段落揭露使用細節（提示詞、受影響的段落）、標明使用者與模型版本與時間，並將互動紀錄作為補充材料提交。
- English: The foundational paper on disclosure ethics. Argues bans are unenforceable and encourage hidden use; because LLMs lack free will they cannot bear moral or legal responsibility, so neither authorship nor acknowledgement is appropriate — they should be cited as tools. Recommends disclosing prompts and affected text in the introduction or methods, recording user, model version and time, and submitting interaction logs as supplementary material.

### A3. 【必讀】

Lin, Z. (2024). Towards an AI policy framework in scholarly publishing. *Trends in Cognitive Sciences*, *28*(2), 85–88. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tics.2023.12.002

- 中文（未依官方摘要）：發表於認知科學社群的政策評論。檢視主要出版者與期刊的 AI 政策後指出其間的不一致與限制，主張建立連貫、務實且「促成使用」（enabling）而非單純禁止的框架：依 AI 使用的類型與程度區分揭露要求，並將責任明確錨定在人類作者身上。對認知科學研究者最直接相關的一篇。
- English (not based on the official abstract): A policy commentary in the cognitive-science community. Reviews publisher and journal AI policies, identifies inconsistencies and limitations, and calls for a coherent, pragmatic, enabling framework that differentiates disclosure requirements by type and degree of AI use while anchoring accountability firmly with human authors. The most directly relevant piece for cognitive-science researchers.

### A4.

Crawford, J., Purvis, A., Grieve, A., & Taylor, L. (2026). Authorship statement for generative artificial intelligence: Assuring trust and accountability. *Journal of University Teaching and Learning Practice*, *23*(1). https://doi.org/10.53761/v16abt43

- 中文：期刊版的 claim accountability。指出 GenAI 使人類貢獻、工具輔助編輯與工具生成內容之間的界線模糊，造成讀者與審查者的真實性問題。提出六項承諾：具體揭露要求、禁止 GenAI 產生稿件的實質學術貢獻、同儕審查中的人類中心與保密、合成媒體的透明使用條件、方法或分析用 GenAI 時的強制反身性、以及責任不得從具名作者移轉。
- English: A journal-level enactment of claim accountability. Notes that GenAI blurs the boundaries between human contribution, tool-assisted editing and tool-generated content, creating a veracity problem. Sets out six commitments, including specific disclosure, prohibition of GenAI producing the substantive scholarly contribution, human centrality and confidentiality in review, mandatory reflexivity when GenAI enters methods or analysis, and non-transferability of accountability away from named authors.

### A5.

Binz, M., Alaniz, S., Roskies, A., Aczel, B., Bergstrom, C. T., Allen, C., Schad, D., Wulff, D., West, J. D., Zhang, Q., Shiffrin, R. M., Gershman, S. J., Popov, V., Bender, E. M., Marelli, M., Botvinick, M. M., Akata, Z., & Schulz, E. (2025). How should the advancement of large language models affect the practice of science? *Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences*, *122*(5), e2401227121. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2401227121

- 中文：四組科學家的正反辯論。Schulz 等人認為與 LLM 合作和與人類合作者合作沒有根本差異；Bender 等人認為 LLM 常被誤用與過度吹捧，應轉向專門且可解釋的工具；Marelli 等人強調透明歸屬與負責任使用；Botvinick 與 Gershman 主張人類須保留決定科學路線圖的責任。各組並互相回應。適合作為研究所課程的討論材料。
- English: A structured debate among four groups. Schulz et al. see working with LLMs as no different from human collaboration; Bender et al. argue LLMs are overhyped and misused and favour specialised, interpretable tools; Marelli et al. stress transparent attribution and responsible use; Botvinick and Gershman insist humans retain responsibility for the scientific roadmap. Each group replies to the others. Well suited to graduate seminar discussion.

### A6.

Johnson, R. F. (2026). Provenance, not prohibition: A framework for AI in scholarly publishing. *Laryngoscope Investigative Otolaryngology*, *11*(4), e70487. https://doi.org/10.1002/lio2.70487

- 中文：與 van Zoonen 立場最接近的獨立提案。核心風險不是工具使用本身而是「來源可追溯性」（provenance）的喪失：讀者、審查者與編輯必須能追溯主張、引用、分析與詮釋如何產生與驗證。框架四原則：區分能力與責任、重來源甚於文采、避免無法查核的禁令、揭露須配合明確驗證。治理單位是稿件周圍的回饋迴圈，作者、審查者、編輯受同樣義務約束。
- English: An independent proposal closest to van Zoonen's position. The central risk is loss of provenance rather than tool use: readers, reviewers and editors must be able to trace how claims, citations, analyses and interpretations were produced and verified. Four principles: separate capability from responsibility, prioritise provenance over polish, avoid unverifiable bans, and pair disclosure with verification. Governance should target the feedback loop around the manuscript and bind all roles equally.

### A7.

Kaebnick, G. E., Magnus, D. C., Kao, A., Hosseini, M., Resnik, D., Dubljević, V., Rentmeester, C., Gordijn, B., & Cherry, M. J. (2023). Editors' statement on the responsible use of generative AI technologies in scholarly journal publishing. *Hastings Center Report*, *53*(5), 3–6. https://doi.org/10.1002/hast.1507（另有 erratum：https://doi.org/10.1002/hast.1534；同文亦刊於 *American Journal of Bioethics*, https://doi.org/10.1080/15265161.2023.2292437）

- 中文：生命倫理與人文期刊編輯群的聯合聲明。承認 GenAI 可能威脅也可能有助於學術工作的目標，提出作者、審查者、編輯使用 GenAI 的初步建議，並附理由，目的是促成更廣泛的社群討論。
- English: A joint statement by bioethics and humanities journal editors. Acknowledges that GenAI may both threaten and serve the goals of scholarly work, and offers a preliminary, reasoned set of recommendations for authors, reviewers and editors to foster a wider conversation.

### A8.

Flanagin, A., Bibbins-Domingo, K., Berkwits, M., & Christiansen, S. L. (2023). Nonhuman "authors" and implications for the integrity of scientific publication and medical knowledge. *JAMA*, *329*(8), 637–639. https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.2023.1344

- 中文：JAMA 編輯群在 ChatGPT 被列為共同作者的事件後發表的社論，確立「非人類不得為作者」與揭露要求的早期基準；是最常被引用的期刊政策文獻之一，作為歷史脈絡引用。
- English: The JAMA editorial issued after ChatGPT appeared as a bylined author, establishing the early baseline that nonhuman entities cannot be authors and that AI use must be disclosed. One of the most cited policy documents; useful as historical context.

### A9.

LaFlamme, M., & Meyers, N. (2026). Enacting AI disclosure in scholarly publishing. *American Ethnologist*, *53*(2), 132–135. https://doi.org/10.1111/amet.70084

- 中文：從人類學出發的評論，描繪新興的 AI 揭露與歸屬實務，討論如何配合該學科的認識論與倫理承諾，並將揭露要求置於跨部門、跨尺度的規範趨勢中。對思考「揭露」在不同學科如何落地有參考價值。
- English: A commentary from anthropology sketching emerging AI disclosure and attribution practices, adapting them to the discipline's epistemic and ethical commitments, and situating disclosure expectations within broader regulatory and professional trends. Useful for thinking about how disclosure is enacted across disciplines.

### A10.

Colangelo, M. T., & Galli, C. (2026). The sophist in the server. *EMBO Reports*, *27*(5), 1098–1102. https://doi.org/10.1038/s44319-026-00711-w

- 中文（依簡短摘要）：LLM 能以同等雄辯為一個假設及其相反辯護，帶來新的科學推理風險：把修辭上的可信與證據支持混為一談。文章分析 LLM 如何重塑研究中的創造、判斷與責任，以及科學家如何利用其流暢性而不放棄認識論紀律。
- English (based on a brief abstract): LLMs can defend a hypothesis and its opposite with equal eloquence, creating a new risk of confusing rhetorical plausibility with evidential support. Analyses how LLMs reshape creativity, judgment and responsibility in research and how scientists can use their fluency without surrendering epistemic discipline.

### A 補充：期刊政策的實證盤點（依重要性排序）

Ganjavi, C., Eppler, M. B., Pekcan, A., Biedermann, B., Abreu, A., Collins, G. S., Gill, I. S., & Cacciamani, G. E. (2024). Publishers' and journals' instructions to authors on use of generative artificial intelligence in academic and scientific publishing: Bibliometric analysis. *BMJ*, *384*, e077192. https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj-2023-077192

- 中文：橫斷面書目計量研究，檢視前 100 大出版者與前 100 高排名期刊的 GenAI 指引。有指引的出版者僅 24%，期刊則 87%；有指引者中 96–98% 禁止 AI 列為作者，但只有一家期刊明文禁止用 GenAI 生成稿件。是引用「期刊實際怎麼規定」時的標準文獻。
- English: A cross-sectional bibliometric study of GenAI guidance from the top 100 publishers and top 100 journals. Only 24% of publishers but 87% of journals provided guidance; among those, 96–98% prohibited AI authorship, yet only one journal explicitly banned GenAI in manuscript generation. The standard reference for what journals actually require.

Perkins, M., & Roe, J. (2024). Academic publisher guidelines on AI usage: A ChatGPT supported thematic analysis. *F1000Research*, *12*, 1398. https://doi.org/10.12688/f1000research.142411.2

- 中文：以 AI 輔助與人工並行的歸納式主題分析，整理主要出版者的政策。共識是人類作者身分至上、GenAI 可用但須揭露；同時討論 AI 輔助分析本身的限制與偏誤。
- English: An inductive thematic analysis, run both with and without AI assistance, of leading publishers' policies. Consensus: human authorship remains paramount and GenAI use is permissible but must be disclosed; also reflects on the limits and biases of AI-assisted analysis itself.

Raitskaya, L., & Tikhonova, E. (2025). The 2025 landscape of generative AI in scholarly writing and publishing: A scoping review of uses and ethical approaches. *Journal of Language and Education*, *11*(4), 5–51. https://doi.org/10.17323/jle.2025.29876

- 中文：依 PRISMA-ScR 的範疇性回顧，納入 2025 年 56 篇文獻。論述集中於治理（作者身分與政策）、技術影響（內容品質）、風險緩解（學術誠信）；並指出分級揭露模型與任務型 AI 分類（如 GAIDeT）等規範框架正在成形。
- English: A PRISMA-ScR scoping review of 56 publications from 2025. Discourse clusters around governance, technological impact and risk mitigation; identifies emerging normative frameworks such as tiered disclosure models and task-based AI taxonomies (e.g., GAIDeT).

Yoo, J. H. (2025). Defining the boundaries of AI use in scientific writing: A comparative review of editorial policies. *Journal of Korean Medical Science*, *40*(23), e187. https://doi.org/10.3346/jkms.2025.40.e187

- 中文：比較國際編輯組織聲明與主要期刊投稿政策，並評估 JKMS 的政策（要求說明工具名稱、提示詞、目的與範圍）。討論 AI 偵測工具的不可靠，主張不應單靠偵測做編輯決定，而應培養負責任揭露的文化。
- English: Compares statements from international editors' organisations and major journals' submission policies, and evaluates JKMS's own policy (tool name, prompt, purpose, scope). Discusses the unreliability of AI-detection tools and argues for a culture of responsible disclosure over detection.

Nabavi, A., Safari, F., Shmoury, A. H., Tabet, S., Perdomo-Luna, C., & Celi, L. A. (2026). Artificial intelligence in scholarly peer review: A scoping review of applications, risks, and governance challenges. *International Journal of Medical Informatics*, *214*, 106418. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijmedinf.2026.106418

- 中文：納入 189 筆紀錄的範疇性回顧，區分輔助型（分流、協助）與自主型（產生審查意見、預測）AI。主要風險是稿件送入第三方工具的保密外洩、偏袒菁英機構或男性作者的演算法偏誤、以及學術聲音的同質化。直接對應 van Zoonen 的 confidentiality threshold。
- English: A scoping review of 189 records distinguishing assistive (triage, assistance) from autonomous (review generation, prediction) AI in peer review. Key risks: confidentiality breaches when manuscripts go to third-party tools, algorithmic bias favouring elite institutions or male authors, and homogenisation of scholarly voice. Maps directly onto van Zoonen's confidentiality threshold.

## 主題 B：認識論風險 — 理解的錯覺與信任校準

對應 Tang 的第一項（epistemic detriment / illusion of understanding）。

### B0. 起點文獻

Tang, B. L. (2025). The epistemic downside of using LLM-based generative AI in academic writing. *Publications*, *13*(4), 63. https://doi.org/10.3390/publications13040063

- 中文：撇開學術倫理的違規問題不談，主張 LLM 用於學術寫作有三項內在的認識論缺陷：理解的錯覺造成的認識論損害、認知鈍化或損傷的可能、以及習慣性或情感性的 AI 依賴。這些缺陷需要的是規範之外的自我節制。
- English: Setting ethics violations aside, argues that LLM use in academic writing carries three intrinsic epistemic pitfalls: epistemic detriment via illusions of understanding, potential cognitive dulling, and habitual or emotional AI dependency — which call for restraint beyond regulation.

### B1. 【必讀】

Messeri, L., & Crockett, M. J. (2024). Artificial intelligence and illusions of understanding in scientific research. *Nature*, *627*(8002), 49–58. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-07146-0

- 中文（未依官方摘要）：Tang「理解的錯覺」的直接源頭。作者整理科學家對 AI 的願景（作為神諭、代理人、量化者、仲裁者），指出這些願景之所以吸引人，是因為它們承諾提升生產力與客觀性，但同時會製造理解的錯覺：研究者以為自己理解得比實際更多、以為探索得比實際更廣、以為 AI 比人更客觀。結果是科學單一文化（monoculture）的風險。這是討論 AI 認識論風險時最常被引用的論文。
- English (not based on the official abstract): The direct source of Tang's "illusion of understanding". Surveys scientists' visions of AI (as oracle, surrogate, quant, arbiter) and argues that their appeal — greater productivity and objectivity — comes with illusions of explanatory depth, exploratory breadth and objectivity, risking scientific monocultures. The most cited paper on the epistemic risks of AI in science.

### B2. 【必讀】

Hila, A. (2025). The epistemological consequences of large language models: Rethinking collective intelligence and institutional knowledge. *AI & Society*, *41*(1), 79–97. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00146-025-02426-3

- 中文：建立「集體認識論」的證成理論，區分內在論（反思性）與外在論（可靠性）證成。論證 LLM 只能提供可靠論式（reliabilist）而非反思性證成：它可靠地傳遞其反思基礎已事先由人建立的資訊，但本身不具反思性證成。把反思性知識大規模外包給 LLM，會在集體層次侵蝕理解與證成標準，並提出個人、機構、法規三層的認識論規範。與 Tang 的論證最相近的哲學文獻。
- English: Develops a theory of "collective epistemology" distinguishing internalist (reflective) from externalist (reliabilist) justification. Argues LLMs approximate reliabilism but lack reflective justification, so outsourcing reflective knowledge to them at scale erodes comprehension and justificatory standards collectively; proposes epistemic norms at individual, institutional and legislative tiers. The philosophical paper closest to Tang's argument.

### B3.

Loru, E., Nudo, J., Di Marco, N., Santirocchi, A., Atzeni, R., Cinelli, M., Cestari, V., Rossi-Arnaud, C., & Quattrociocchi, W. (2025). The simulation of judgment in LLMs. *Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences*, *122*(42), e2518443122. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2518443122

- 中文：以六個 LLM 對照專家評分與人類受試者的判斷，在相同的評估程序下比較。輸出雖然一致，模型依賴的可觀察判準卻與人不同，反映詞彙關聯與統計先驗而非脈絡推理，並出現政治不對稱與「把語言形式當成認識論可靠性」的傾向，作者稱之為 epistemia：表面可信取代驗證時產生的知識錯覺。
- English: Benchmarks six LLMs against expert ratings and human judgments under an identical evaluation procedure. Despite aligned outputs, models rely on different observable criteria — lexical associations and statistical priors rather than contextual reasoning — showing political asymmetries and a tendency to confuse linguistic form with epistemic reliability, termed *epistemia*: the illusion of knowledge when surface plausibility replaces verification.

### B4.

Heersmink, R., de Rooij, B., Clavel Vázquez, M. J., & Colombo, M. (2024). A phenomenology and epistemology of large language models: Transparency, trust, and trustworthiness. *Ethics and Information Technology*, *26*(3), 41. https://doi.org/10.1007/s10676-024-09777-3

- 中文：把 LLM 概念化為多功能的計算認知人工物。現象學上 LLM 可被體驗為「準他者」而被擬人化；它們在資料與演算法層面不透明，但在現象與資訊層面卻透明流暢。這種組合讓使用者難以正確校準信任，導致對輸出（尤其是幻覺）的不當信任，也削弱 LLM 本身的可信度。
- English: Conceptualises LLMs as multifunctional computational cognitive artifacts. Phenomenologically they can be experienced as a "quasi-other" and anthropomorphised; they are opaque in data and algorithm yet phenomenologically and informationally transparent. This combination makes trust calibration difficult, producing unwarranted trust (especially when models hallucinate) and undermining trustworthiness.

### B5.

Kim, S. S. Y., Vaughan, J. W., Liao, Q. V., Lombrozo, T., & Russakovsky, O. (2025). Fostering appropriate reliance on large language models: The role of explanations, sources, and inconsistencies. *Proceedings of the 2025 CHI Conference on Human Factors in Computing Systems*, 1–19. https://doi.org/10.1145/3706598.3714020

- 中文：先以放聲思考研究找出影響依賴的回應特徵，再以預先註冊的受控實驗（N = 308）分離其效果。結果：有解釋會提高對正確與錯誤回答的依賴；提供來源或解釋出現不一致時，對錯誤回答的依賴才降低。對「AI 附上理由就更可信」的直覺是重要的實證警訊。
- English: A think-aloud study followed by a pre-registered controlled experiment (N = 308). Explanations increase reliance on both correct and incorrect responses; reliance on incorrect responses drops only when sources are provided or explanations are inconsistent. An important empirical caution against the intuition that AI reasoning makes outputs more trustworthy.

### B6.

Bottazzi Grifoni, E., & Ferrario, R. (2025). The bewitching AI: The illusion of communication with large language models. *Philosophy & Technology*, *38*(2), 61. https://doi.org/10.1007/s13347-025-00893-6

- 中文：以後期維根斯坦的語言觀分析人與 LLM 的互動。成功的溝通需要在定義與判斷上維持參照點的恆常性，而 LLM 無法在對話中追蹤否定與矛盾。表面的理解來自「蠱惑」：LLM 對語言模式的統計依循，加上人類盲目跟隨熟悉語言遊戲的傾向。LLM 越能模擬刻板互動，錯覺只會越深。
- English: Analyses human–LLM interaction through late Wittgenstein. Communication requires constancy of reference points in definitions and judgments, which LLMs cannot maintain across negations and contradictions. Apparent understanding is a "bewitchment" produced by LLMs' statistical adherence to linguistic patterns and humans' tendency to follow familiar language games; better mimicry only deepens the illusion.

### B7.

Abdurahman, S., Atari, M., Karimi-Malekabadi, F., Xue, M. J., Trager, J., Park, P. S., Golazizian, P., Omrani, A., & Dehghani, M. (2024). Perils and opportunities in using large language models in psychological research. *PNAS Nexus*, *3*(7), pgae245. https://doi.org/10.1093/pnasnexus/pgae245

- 中文：針對心理學研究的「GPTology」警告。以實證研究展示在零樣本設定下把 LLM 當作通用文本分析工具或人類心智模型的風險，強調全球心理多樣性、透明開放的方法、以及避免同質化與過度依賴。對使用 LLM 做文本標註或模擬受試者的研究者最實用。
- English: A caution against "GPTology" in psychological research. Demonstrates empirically the risks of treating LLMs (especially zero-shot) as universal text-analysis tools or models of the psyche, stressing global psychological diversity, transparent open methods, and resistance to homogenisation and over-reliance. Most practical for researchers using LLMs for annotation or simulated participants.

### B8.

Maynard, A. D. (2026). *The AI cognitive Trojan horse: How large language models may bypass human epistemic vigilance* [Preprint]. arXiv. https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2601.07085

- 中文：預印本。借用 Sperber 等人的「認識論警覺」理論，主張對話式 AI 的主要風險不在錯誤或欺騙，而在它呈現「誠實的非訊號」：流暢、樂於助人、看似中立等特徵在人類身上代價高昂因而有訊息價值，在 LLM 身上卻近乎免費。提出四種繞過機制，並推測認知越精熟的使用者可能越容易受影響。
- English: Preprint. Drawing on Sperber et al.'s epistemic vigilance, argues the main risk of conversational AI is not error or deception but "honest non-signals": fluency, helpfulness and apparent disinterest that carry information in humans (where they are costly) but are computationally trivial for LLMs. Identifies four bypass mechanisms and speculates that cognitively sophisticated users may be more vulnerable.

### B9.

Ibrahim, L., Collins, K. M., Kim, S. S. Y., Reuel, A., Lamparth, M., Feng, K., Ahmad, L., Soni, P., El Kattan, A., Stein, M., Swaroop, S., Padmakumar, V., Sucholutsky, I., Strait, A., Yang, D., Liao, Q. V., & Bhatt, U. (2025). *Measuring and mitigating overreliance to build human-compatible AI* [Preprint]. arXiv. https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2509.08010

- 中文：預印本。主張測量與減緩「過度依賴」應成為 LLM 研究與部署的核心。整理個人與社會層次的風險（高風險錯誤、治理挑戰、認知去技能化），分析導致過度依賴的模型特性、系統設計與使用者認知偏誤，指出既有測量方法的三個缺口，並提出緩解策略。
- English: Preprint. Argues that measuring and mitigating overreliance must be central to LLM research and deployment. Consolidates individual and societal risks (high-stakes errors, governance challenges, cognitive deskilling), examines model characteristics, design features and user biases that drive overreliance, identifies three measurement gaps, and proposes mitigation strategies.

## 主題 C：認知鈍化、認知卸載與依賴

對應 Tang 的第二、三項（cognitive dulling / AI dependency）。

### C1. 【必讀】

Lee, H.-P., Sarkar, A., Tankelevitch, L., Drosos, I., Rintel, S., Banks, R., & Wilson, N. (2025). The impact of generative AI on critical thinking: Self-reported reductions in cognitive effort and confidence effects from a survey of knowledge workers. *Proceedings of the 2025 CHI Conference on Human Factors in Computing Systems*, 1–22. https://doi.org/10.1145/3706598.3713778

- 中文（未依官方摘要）：對知識工作者的問卷調查，蒐集實際使用 GenAI 的任務案例。主要發現：對 GenAI 越有信心者，自陳投入的批判思考越少；對自身能力越有信心者則越多。GenAI 使批判思考的性質從資訊蒐集轉為資訊驗證、從問題解決轉為 AI 回應的整合、從任務執行轉為任務監督。是目前被引用最多的「AI 與批判思考」實證研究之一。
- English (not based on the official abstract): A survey of knowledge workers reporting real GenAI-assisted tasks. Higher confidence in GenAI was associated with less self-reported critical thinking, higher self-confidence with more. GenAI shifts critical thinking from information gathering to verification, from problem-solving to integrating AI responses, and from task execution to task stewardship. Among the most cited empirical studies on AI and critical thinking.

### C2. 【必讀】

Fan, Y., Tang, L., Le, H., Shen, K., Tan, S., Zhao, Y., Shen, Y., Li, X., & Gašević, D. (2025). Beware of metacognitive laziness: Effects of generative artificial intelligence on learning motivation, processes, and performance. *British Journal of Educational Technology*, *56*(2), 489–530. https://doi.org/10.1111/bjet.13544

- 中文：隨機實驗，117 名大學生在寫作任務中分別獲得 ChatGPT、人類專家、寫作分析工具或無支援。各組動機無差異；自我調節學習歷程的頻率與序列有顯著差異；ChatGPT 組的作文分數進步最多，但知識獲得與遷移沒有比較好。作者以「後設認知怠惰」描述這種表現提升與學習脫鉤的現象。
- English: A randomised experiment with 117 university students supported by ChatGPT, a human expert, writing-analytics tools or nothing. No differences in motivation; significant differences in self-regulated learning processes; the ChatGPT group improved essay scores most but showed no advantage in knowledge gain or transfer. The authors call this decoupling of performance from learning "metacognitive laziness".

### C3.

Gerlich, M. (2025). AI tools in society: Impacts on cognitive offloading and the future of critical thinking. *Societies*, *15*(1), 6. https://doi.org/10.3390/soc15010006（勘誤：https://doi.org/10.3390/soc15090252）

- 中文：混合方法研究，666 名受試者的問卷與深度訪談。頻繁使用 AI 工具與批判思考能力呈顯著負相關，由認知卸載中介；年輕受試者依賴度較高、批判思考分數較低；教育程度較高者批判思考較佳。被引用極廣，但為相關性設計，因果推論須謹慎，且有一則表格勘誤。
- English: A mixed-methods study (surveys and interviews, N = 666). Frequent AI-tool use correlated negatively with critical-thinking ability, mediated by cognitive offloading; younger participants showed higher dependence and lower scores; higher education predicted better critical thinking. Very widely cited, but correlational — causal claims should be cautious — and carries a table correction.

### C4.

Kosmyna, N., Hauptmann, E., Yuan, Y. T., Situ, J., Liao, X.-H., Beresnitzky, A. V., Braunstein, I., & Maes, P. (2025). *Your brain on ChatGPT: Accumulation of cognitive debt when using an AI assistant for essay writing task* [Preprint]. arXiv. https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2506.08872

- 中文：預印本。54 名受試者分為 LLM、搜尋引擎、純腦力三組各寫三次作文，第四次交換條件（18 人完成）。EEG 顯示純腦力組的腦網路連結最強且最分散，LLM 組最弱；LLM 轉純腦力者 alpha 與 beta 連結降低；LLM 組的作文擁有感最低，也最難正確引述自己的文字。作者以「認知債務」概括。已有方法學評論（Stanković et al., 2026, https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2601.00856），引用時宜一併參考。
- English: Preprint. Fifty-four participants wrote essays in LLM, search-engine or brain-only conditions over three sessions, with a fourth crossover session (n = 18). EEG showed the strongest, most distributed connectivity in the brain-only group and the weakest in the LLM group; LLM-to-brain participants showed reduced alpha and beta connectivity; LLM users reported the lowest essay ownership and struggled to quote their own work. Framed as "cognitive debt". A methodological commentary exists (Stanković et al., 2026) and should be read alongside.

### C5.

Yan, L., Greiff, S., Lodge, J. M., & Gašević, D. (2025). Distinguishing performance gains from learning when using generative AI. *Nature Reviews Psychology*, *4*(7), 435–436. https://doi.org/10.1038/s44159-025-00467-5

- 中文：短評，指出 GenAI 在教育中能提升表現，但這類使用並不促進高品質學習所需的深層認知與後設認知處理，主張研究與實務須區分「表現提升」與「學習」。可視為 Fan et al. 的概念總結。
- English: A short comment arguing that although GenAI boosts learners' performance, such uses do not promote the deep cognitive and metacognitive processing that high-quality learning requires; research and practice must distinguish performance gains from learning. A conceptual distillation of Fan et al.

### C6.

Clark, A. (2025). Extending minds with generative AI. *Nature Communications*, *16*(1), 4627. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-59906-9

- 中文：延伸心智取徑的反面聲音。主張建立混合思考系統、流暢納入非生物資源本是人類本性，認清這一點會改變我們看待 AI 威脅與承諾的方式。放在此處作為對「認知卸載必然有害」的平衡。
- English: The counter-voice from the extended-mind tradition. Argues it is basic human nature to build hybrid thinking systems that incorporate non-biological resources, and that recognising this reframes both the threats and promises of AI. Included as a balance to the assumption that offloading is inherently harmful.

### C7.

Delikoura, I., Fung, Y. R., & Hui, P. (2025). *From superficial outputs to superficial learning: Risks of large language models in education* [Preprint]. arXiv. https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2509.21972

- 中文：預印本。系統性回顧 70 篇跨電腦科學、教育與心理學的實證研究。模型層次的風險（膚淺理解、偏誤、幻覺、擬人化等）在學習者互動中延伸為認知與行為後果：神經活動降低、過度依賴、獨立學習能力下降、學生能動性喪失。提出 LLM-Risk Adapted Learning Model 描述風險如何層層傳遞。
- English: Preprint. A systematic review of 70 empirical studies across computer science, education and psychology. Model-level risks (superficial understanding, bias, hallucination, anthropomorphism) cascade through learner interaction into cognitive and behavioural outcomes: reduced neural activity, over-reliance, diminished independent learning and loss of agency. Proposes an LLM-Risk Adapted Learning Model.

### C8.

Choudhury, A., & Chaudhry, Z. (2024). Large language models and user trust: Consequence of self-referential learning loop and the deskilling of health care professionals. *Journal of Medical Internet Research*, *26*, e56764. https://doi.org/10.2196/56764

- 中文：探討臨床人員對 LLM 的信任、AI 生成內容回流訓練資料的自我參照迴圈、以及對專業能力的影響。強調專家使用者可卸載例行工作但須維持批判監督，並警告頻繁依賴會導致診斷與思考技能的去技能化，尤其影響受訓中的專業人員。
- English: Examines clinician trust in LLMs, the self-referential loop in which AI-generated content feeds back into training data, and consequences for competence. Stresses that expert users can offload routine tasks while maintaining critical oversight, and warns that frequent reliance risks deskilling diagnostic and reasoning abilities, particularly for trainees.

### C9.

Izquierdo-Condoy, J. S., Arias-Intriago, M., Tello-De-la-Torre, A., Busch, F., & Ortiz-Prado, E. (2025). Generative artificial intelligence in medical education: Enhancing critical thinking or undermining cognitive autonomy? *Journal of Medical Internet Research*, *27*, e76340. https://doi.org/10.2196/76340

- 中文：觀點文章，評估 GenAI 對醫學教育中批判思考的影響。既有證據好壞參半：投入度提升，但也有被動化與易受錯誤資訊影響的風險。主張若無強調倫理使用、提示工程與批判評估的課程整合，GenAI 可能損害學生的認知自主。
- English: A viewpoint assessing GenAI's effect on critical thinking in medical education. Evidence is mixed: greater engagement alongside passivity and susceptibility to misinformation. Argues that without curricular integration emphasising ethical use, prompt engineering and critical evaluation, GenAI may compromise students' cognitive autonomy.

- English: A Delphi study with 72 researchers in AI and digitisation.
